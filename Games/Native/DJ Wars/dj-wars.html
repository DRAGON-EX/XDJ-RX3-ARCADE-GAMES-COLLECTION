HOW TO PLAY

One 2640 × 1600 battlefield with a scrolling camera and tactical overview. Five fighters per side. Your team: DJ-DRAGONX the dragon and Yuki, Peach, Cookie and Mochi. The enemy mouse-helmet crew is controlled by the game; its boss has 180 HP. You have 90 seconds each turn.

ARROWS: move left/right and aim up/down. ENTER: jump. SPACE: hold to charge, release to fire. 1–6: equipment. T: cycle targets. Click the battlefield to mark an airstrike or homing destination. One attack causes at most 50 HP damage per fighter, including the airstrike salvo and blast-induced falls. Friendly fire is active, and falling into the water eliminates a fighter.

Airstrikes launch immediately. Bazookas are unlimited; other weapons have shared team ammunition.

LOWER LEVEL: Mochi and Deadbeat 3 each have two teleports and two jetpacks. TELEPORT: select 5, click clear space above your destination, then SPACE. JETPACK: select 6, SPACE to start, arrows to fly; SPACE again stops it. Fuel lasts ten seconds. Teleport ends your turn; using a jetpack keeps the turn running. Large falls cause damage; small jumps are safe.

Drag the battlefield to pan, click the mini-map to look elsewhere, M toggles the whole-map overview, and C follows your fighter or projectile. P: pause. R: restart. ESC: launcher. Browser preview of our shared native C game. RX3 hardware controls and measured device performance are still pending.
